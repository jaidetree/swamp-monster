# PRD: Swamp Monster Leather

Marketing site for Swamp Monster Leather, a leather goods maker, built on
Phoenix (dark-mode only, no light/dark toggle).

## Goals

- Present the brand and finished work (bags, goods) to visitors.
- Let visitors reach out via a contact form, routed to email.
- Showcase a dedicated works/portfolio page.
- Stand up lightweight CMS contexts so site content (works, training,
  resources) can be managed without hardcoding markup.

## Pages

- **Home** (`/`) — hero, works showcase, footer sections for training,
  resources, and about.
- **Contact** (`/contact`) — form that emails the Swamp Monster inbox.
- **Works** (`/works`, planned) — full portfolio listing beyond the home page
  teaser.

## Out of scope (for now)

- Auth/accounts — no user-facing login.
- E-commerce/checkout.
